THE QUESTION

What does our digital behavior reveal about us?

Social media has become part of everyday life, but the way we use these platforms varies across people, demographics, and online communities.

The Need for Feed explores patterns across social media usage, platform behavior, and self-reported mental health experiences to better understand what the data can reveal about our digital habits.

01

Who is using social media, and how does usage differ across demographics?

02

What patterns emerge between time spent online and reported mental health experiences?

03

How do behaviors and experiences differ across social media platforms?

EXPLORING THE DATA

From survey responses to interactive insights.

Interactive Tableau visualizations let users move beyond static results and explore patterns across demographics, daily usage, and social media platforms.

01

Social Media Usage

Explore how social media usage varies across platforms and demographic groups, revealing differences in how people engage with the online landscape.

02

Mental Health & Daily Usage

Compare reported feelings of anxiety and depression across different levels of daily social media use and explore how responses shift as time online increases.

03

Platform Behavior

Compare behavioral patterns across social media platforms and explore how measures such as distractibility, validation, comparison, and restlessness vary across different online communities.

PERSONALIZED EXPLORATION

See where you fit within the data.

Users can select their age group, gender, and daily social media usage to compare their profile with responses from the broader dataset, making the findings more personal and easier to explore.

01

Select your profile

Choose an age group, gender, and level of daily social media use.

02

Compare responses

View broader respondent distributions alongside results for your selected demographic profile.

03

Explore the difference

Examine how reported anxiety and depression patterns vary across different groups and usage levels.

TESTING & ITERATION

Designing for the people behind the data.

After developing the interactive experience, we conducted usability testing with friends and family to understand how easily users could navigate the site, interpret the visualizations, and interact with the data. Their feedback helped us identify areas for improvement and refine the final experience.

01

Test

Observed users navigating the website and interacting with the Tableau visualizations.

02

Learn

Gathered feedback on navigation, clarity, usability, and how easily users could understand the data story.

03

Refine

Used feedback from testing to make adjustments and improve the final interactive experience.
