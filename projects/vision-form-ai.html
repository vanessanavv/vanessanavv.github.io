VisionFormAI

An end-to-end AI web application that analyzes movement in real time, delivers personalized feedback, and tracks progress over time.

THE PROBLEM

People want to be healthier, but don't know where to start.

Building healthier habits can be difficult without clear, personalized feedback. While exercise resources are widely available, knowing how you're moving, where you can improve, and whether you're making progress is much harder.

VisionFormAI explores how computer vision can make movement feedback more accessible by turning exercise into measurable, personalized insights that users can understand and track over time.

From movement to meaningful feedback.
