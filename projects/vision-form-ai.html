KEY LEARNINGS & IMPACT

What we learned building VisionFormAI.

Building and testing the platform revealed lessons that shaped both the technical implementation and the user experience.

01

Real-time computer vision can work in the browser.

Movement assessment can run directly from a standard device camera without backend compute for pose detection. Lighting, camera position, and background conditions still affect landmark reliability and must be considered in the experience.

02

Personalization becomes more valuable over time.

Rather than treating each assessment as an isolated result, VisionFormAI uses a personal baseline and progress tracking to make changes across sessions visible and meaningful.

03

Good analysis still needs good communication.

Translating raw movement data into clear scores, movement-specific feedback, and understandable visual breakdowns made the results more useful and actionable.

04

The concept can extend beyond general fitness.

The same approach could potentially support use cases where changes in movement quality matter over time, including aging adults and individuals progressing through rehabilitation.
